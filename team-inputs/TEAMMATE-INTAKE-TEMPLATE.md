## Part 3 — Your loops

A loop is a set of links that returns to where it started.

- **Reinforcing (R)** — the loop amplifies itself. Growth, or decline that feeds on itself.
- **Balancing (B)** — the loop pushes back toward a limit. It stabilizes, resists, or caps.

*(Balancing does not mean good, and reinforcing does not mean bad.)*

## How your input gets used

Each submission is merged into a shared map without being flattened into consensus. Every item is tagged with three independent labels:

| Label | Says what |
|---|---|
| **Epistemic status** | Supported · Inferred · Assumed · Contested · Unresolved |
| **Claim basis** | Evidence · Analytical inference · Group convergence · Model definition |
| **Decision status** | Active direction · Hypothesis · Intended outcome · Unresolved |

The rule the map enforces: **team agreement is not evidence.** If three of us independently propose the same loop, that is recorded as *group convergence* — a meaningful signal about our shared reading of the system, but it does not promote the claim to *supported*. Where we genuinely disagree, the disagreement is preserved and shown as a divergence rather than averaged away.
